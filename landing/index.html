Built to be fast, private, and work everywhere.

100% Private

Universal

Browser extension for Chrome, Edge & Firefox. Desktop app for Mac & Windows. Web app for iOS & Android.

Lightning Fast

Selective Scan

Drag-to-select means you scan exactly the QR code you want, even on busy pages.

Keeps your last 50 scans. Click any item to re-open or copy. Configurable in settings.

Fully Customizable

Change the keyboard shortcut, toggle auto-open, notifications, and history size.

How it works

Three simple steps to scan any QR code on your screen.

1

Press the shortcut

Cmd+Shift+Y on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+Y on Windows. The screen dims with a crosshair cursor.

2

Drag to select

Draw a rectangle around the QR code. The selected area stays bright while the rest is dimmed.

3

Done!

URLs open automatically in your browser. Text is copied to your clipboard. That's it.

Your privacy is protected

QR Scan & Open processes everything locally on your device. No images, URLs, or scan data are ever sent to any server. Open source and fully transparent.
